---
name: Email design direction
description: Emails should use a cleaner, more premium structure with consistent hierarchy, cards, and CTA blocks.
type: design
---
All auth and app emails should feel visually consistent with the Serviceydere brand.
Use a dark header, white body, soft slate cards, teal primary CTA, clearer section hierarchy, and more structured information blocks.
Prefer concise hero intro blocks, separated content cards, and cleaner footer/help sections.
